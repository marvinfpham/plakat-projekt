# Approval policy (implementation gate)

Development work, draft copy, local previews and reversible tests can proceed within the approved Stage B architecture. Every artwork version requires owner approval before product publication. The approval record must identify the artwork digest/version, exact action, expected cost, approver, time and scope. A material artwork edit invalidates the approval.

Store publication, paid subscription, domain purchase, sample order, live advertising, refund outside approved policy and production integration writes need a separate specific approval. No standing spending authorization exists. Production order fulfillment cannot run unattended until verification, deduplication, retry bounds, reconciliation, alerting and emergency stop are tested.
